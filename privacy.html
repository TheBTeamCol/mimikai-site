Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Overview

Mimikai is a team charades game. To create and play a game, the app connects to our server, which sets up your session, serves clues, and keeps score. We collect only what is needed for the game to work: you do not need to create an account or provide your name, email, or phone number.

This policy describes what data is processed, what we do not collect, and how third-party services are involved. It is written to align with the Apple App Store Review Guidelines and the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

Information we process

When you create and play a game, the app sends the following game configuration and session data to our server:

  • Team names — labels you choose (for example, "Team 1")
  • Color and number of players for each team
  • Age range for each team
  • Country and language you selected
  • Categories you chose (movies, animals, professions, etc.)
  • Game progress and scores during the session

This data is used solely to build your game, show appropriate clues, keep score, and let you resume a game in progress. All communication with our server is encrypted over HTTPS.

Team names are free text that you type. We recommend not using real names or other personal information as a team name, since they are stored together with the game.

Data we do not collect

Mimikai does not harvest, request, or collect the following:

  • Personal contact information (legal name, email address, physical address, or phone number) — unless you voluntarily email us for support
  • User accounts or logins (the current version does not require them)
  • Real-time or historical geolocation data
  • Contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or other device data unrelated to gameplay
  • Health, medical, or biometric identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or AAID) for profiling or tracking purposes

Clue generation (AI)

When our database does not have enough clues for your configuration, we generate new ones using a large language model provider (OpenAI and/or Google Gemini). Those requests contain only game parameters — category, age range, country, and language — and never personal data or your team names.

Generated clues are stored in our database and reused in future games to improve the catalog. These clues contain no personal information. See the privacy policies of OpenAI and Google for more details on how they handle data.

Storage and retention

Game data is stored in our database to run and restore your session. Generated clues are retained as part of the shared game catalog. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

Mimikai relies on the following external services:

  • AI providers — OpenAI and/or Google Gemini, solely to generate clues when needed.
  • Server infrastructure — cloud hosting where our backend runs the game API.

Mimikai does not integrate third-party advertising or behavioral tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Mimikai is meant to be played in groups, including with children, and filters clue content by each team's age range. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the game requires no accounts and no personal data, no information that directly identifies a child is collected.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has entered personal information as a team name or otherwise in a game session, please contact us and we will help remove the relevant data where possible.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
