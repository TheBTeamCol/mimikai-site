Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Overview

Mimikai is a team charades game. To work, the app connects to our server, which sets up and runs your game and generates the clues. We collect only the minimum needed for the game to function: you don't need to create an account or give us your name, email, or phone number.

Information we process

When you create and play a game, the app sends the game configuration to our server:

  • Team names — the labels you choose (for example, "Team 1")
  • Color and number of players for each team
  • Age range for each team
  • Country and language you selected
  • Categories you chose (movies, jobs, etc.)
  • Game progress and scores

This data is used solely to build your game, show the right clues, keep score, and let you resume a game in progress.

Team names are free text that you type. We recommend not using full real names or personal data as a team name, since they are stored together with the game.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Accounts or logins (the current version does not require them)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or other device data
  • Advertising identifiers

Clue generation (AI)

When our database doesn't have enough clues for your configuration, we generate them using a large language model provider (OpenAI or Google Gemini). Those requests contain only game parameters — category, age range, country, and language — and never personal data or your team names.

Generated clues are stored in our database and reused in future games to improve the catalog. These clues contain no personal information. See the privacy policies of OpenAI and Google for more details on how they handle data.

Storage and retention

Game data is stored in our database to run and restore the game. Generated clues are retained as part of the game's catalog. Information is transmitted encrypted over HTTPS.

Third-party services

Mimikai relies on the following external services:

  • AI providers — OpenAI and/or Google Gemini, solely to generate clues.
  • Server infrastructure — where the backend that runs the games is hosted.

Mimikai does not integrate third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Mimikai is meant to be played in groups, including with children, and filters clue content by each team's age range. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from minors. Because the game requires no accounts or personal data, no information that identifies a child is collected.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
