Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Overview

MIMIK-AI is designed with privacy at its core. Your privacy is our highest priority, and this policy outlines our strict commitment to transparency under standard mobile industry frameworks—including Google Firebase guidelines, the Apple App Store Review Guidelines, and the Google Play Developer Program Policies. Your data stays on your devices — we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

Data We Do Not Collect

MIMIK-AI explicitly does not harvest, request, or collect the following information:

  • Personal information (such as your legal name, email address, physical address, or phone number)
  • Real-time or historical geolocation data
  • Health, medical, or biometric identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or AAID) for profiling or tracking purposes

Data Stored on Your Device

The following categories of data are processed and stored locally on your smartphone or compatible wearable devices, remaining strictly within your application's sandboxed environment:

  • Schedules — your specific custom intervals, notifications, and interaction routines configured within the App.
  • Preferences — user interface configurations, notification rules, sound cues, and local visual arrangements.
  • Activity Counters — local tracking metrics, completed games, and performance summaries computed directly on your device.

Any synchronization between paired systems (such as a smartphone and a companion smartwatch) utilizes native, encrypted, device-to-device wireless protocols (such as Apple's WatchConnectivity framework or Android's Wearable Data Layer API). This data is never sent to an external server.

Subscriptions and Billing

MIMIK-AI offers a subscription model priced at $4.99 USD per month. All financial transactions, billing cycles, renewals, and cancellations are processed entirely and exclusively through Apple's App Store (using StoreKit) or Google's Play Store billing platform.

  • We do not have access to, nor do we collect, process, or store your credit card data, bank details, or payment configurations.
  • Subscription management is handled directly by the operating system account settings of your device.

Third-Party Services and Infrastructure

To optimize application stability, track technical health, and guarantee a seamless user experience, MIMIK-AI integrates select components of the Google Firebase suite. These integrations strictly adhere to data-minimization practices:

  • Firebase Crashlytics & Performance Monitoring: We use these tools to receive anonymized, aggregated system crash logs and bug data. These reports only capture stack traces, device hardware models, and operating system variations. No personally identifiable information (PII) is attached to these logs.
  • StoreKit / Google Play Billing: The only external platform services involved in the app architecture are the native app store APIs used strictly for secure subscription authentication.

Children's Privacy

MIMIK-AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, store, or process personal data from children under 13. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) youth provisions, if a parent or guardian becomes aware that a minor has bypassed verification or generated local diagnostic records, please contact us immediately so we can facilitate clearing any localized configurations or cached profiles.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. If we make changes to this documentation, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued deployment and interaction with MIMIK-AI after such modifications constitutes your acknowledgement and formal acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
